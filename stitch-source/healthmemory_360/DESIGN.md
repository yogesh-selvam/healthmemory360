## Brand & Style

### Visual Personality & Target Audience
The design system establishes a calm, clinical-grade, yet distinctly human digital environment. Crafted for health-conscious individuals, chronic care navigators, and caregivers, the system prioritizes clarity, cognitive ease, and computational transparency over decoration. It rejects outdated sterile hospital palettes, cartoon clinical illustrations, and synthetic chatbot gimmicks in favor of a modern, quiet-tech authority.

### Design Movement
The visual style operates at the intersection of **Clinical Minimalism** and **Modern Soft-Tech**:
- **Generous Whitespace & Structural Quietude**: Breathing room is utilized as a cognitive buffer to counter health anxiety.
- **Micro-Contrast & Precision Borders**: Flat surfaces accented by razor-thin, semi-transparent borders anchor dense medical values without heavy box shadows.
- **Intelligent Accentuation**: Purposeful chromatic coding distinguishes personal health history, vital stability, and AI computational insights.
- **Decoupled Data Architecture**: Numbers and clinical metrics take precedence over decorative visuals, treated with architectural clarity.

## Colors

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#0F172A` / `#0B1324`)**: Deep Midnight Slate. Grounds core navigation, major display typography, high-priority icons, and structural anchor surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` / `#14B8A6`)**: Clinical Teal. Acts as the primary interactive catalyst for high-intent actions, active navigation tab indicators, primary toggles, and data focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6`)**: Intelligence Violet. Reserved exclusively for AI processing, document parsing tags, synthesized intelligence badges, and source citation chips.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` / `#334155` / `#F8FAFC`)**: Functional Slate palette spanning canvas backdrops, secondary body text, and architectural 1px divider structures.

### Functional & Semantic Application
- **Health Trend System**: Positive changes and stable vitals leverage `mint-wellness` (`#10B981`) combined with light tint backings (`#ECFDF5`), warning thresholds rely on Amber (`#F59E0B`), and acute deviations use Urgent Red (`#EF4444`).
- **AI Chromatic Isolation**: AI elements never share the teal interactive shade. Purple is deliberately contained to distinguish verified machine extraction from manual user logs.
- **Contrast Integrity**: All body and metric pairings maintain a minimum WCAG AAA contrast ratio on light backings (`#F8FAFC` and `#FFFFFF`).

## Typography

### Hierarchy & Type Pairing
- **Manrope (Display & Headlines)**: Chosen for structural titles, section leaders, and quantitative metric values. Its geometric base and open apertures project progressive authority while retaining approachable warmth.
- **Inter (Body, Controls & Labels)**: Chosen for continuous reading, microcopy, table rows, and navigational links. Provides robust optical legibility at micro sizes and handles dense health metrics without crowding.

### Metric-First Typographic Rule
Clinical and wellness figures rely on decoupled typographic construction:
- **Value**: Rendered using `metric-display` with tight tracking (`-0.03em`) and heavy weight (`700`).
- **Unit of Measurement**: Decoupled from the number string, set in `metric-unit`, tinted with `slate-subtext`, baseline-aligned or slightly offset to maintain clarity between numbers and clinical dimensions (e.g., `108` in Manrope 700 + ` mg/dL` in Inter 500).

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
- **Desktop Experience (>= 1024px)**: Fixed-fluid hybrid architecture. A persistent 280px left sidebar anchors primary navigation, complemented by an 80px high top utility app-bar. The main viewport utilizes a 12-column fluid grid bounded by a max-width of `1440px`, with 24px gutters and 48px outer margins.
- **Mobile Experience (< 768px)**: Single-column vertical stream governed by 16px lateral padding. Persistent bottom bar navigation (5 key actions: Home, Timeline, Records, Fitness, AI) paired with a contextual dynamic action trigger.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built upon a strict 4px/8px modular base scale:
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Micro-gaps between badge icons, unit chips, and grouped data rows.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard internal card padding, table cell gaps, and form input insets.
- `space-lg` (24px): Inter-card spacing, module padding, and major subsection intervals.
- `space-xl` (32px) to `space-2xl` (48px): Page section transitions and primary dashboard layout blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Philosophy
The design avoids deep drop shadows, heavy dark drop boundaries, and pronounced Skeuomorphic bevels. Visual depth is maintained through layered tonal surfaces and razor-sharp structural outlines.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#F8FAFC`. Background layer beneath all modules.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on Level 0, bound by a crisp 1px stroke of `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`). Shadow is minimal: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays, Dropdowns & Popovers)**: `#FFFFFF` bounded by `border-subtle` with an ambient diffusion shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Drawers)**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface elevated above a 40% opacity midnight backdrop blur (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`), supported by `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)`.
- **AI Processing Layer**: Translucent violet and teal hairline gradients (`1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`) coupled with soft ambient glow (`0 0 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Curvature Scales
The visual interface employs soft, clean curvature that feels warm and modern without drifting into juvenile or overly playful bubble-like geometries:
- **Base (0.5rem / 8px)**: Form fields, action buttons, table cell selections, dropdown elements, and pagination buttons.
- **Large (1rem / 16px)**: Standard metric containers, list preview cards, report preview blocks, and health summary pods.
- **Extra Large (1.5rem / 24px)**: Hero processing containers, doctor visit brief exports, and main modal overlays.
- **Pill (Full 9999px)**: Status badges, category filters, interactive AI prompt chips, and source citations.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#0D9488` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, font `label-md`. Subtle hover transition to `#0F766E` with an internal focus ring of `2px` offset by `2px` white. Height `44px`, horizontal padding `20px`, shape `0.5rem`.
- **Secondary / Neutral**: Transparent surface, 1px border of `#E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9`.
- **AI Action**: Subtle gradient from `#8B5CF6` to `#7C3AED` with `#FFFFFF` text, prefixed with a refined spark icon.
- **Subtle Destructive**: Tinted surface `#FEF2F2`, `#EF4444` border and text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Health Trend**: Full pill (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 10px`. Stable/Improved vitals utilize `#ECFDF5` background with `#10B981` text. Alert metrics utilize `#FEF2F2` background with `#EF4444` text.
- **AI Citation Pill**: Small footprint (`padding: 3px 8px`, `caption` typography), `#F5F3FF` background, `1px solid #DDD6FE`, `#7C3AED` text. Displays file source and record timestamp.
- **Filter Tags**: Border-driven chips with `#F8FAFC` background, activating to `#0F172A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text on selection.

### Cards & Metric Containers
- **Metric Card**: White base with `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Features a two-tier header (title and contextual menu/source icon), a central decoupled number-unit block, and a bottom row displaying historical trend chips (`+2.4% vs Jun 2025`).
- **Medical Record Card**: Contains document-type thumbnail glyph, document title, extraction status tag (`AI Extracted`), date, and primary `View` and `Compare` micro-actions.

### Health Timeline Node
- **Timeline Spine**: Central or left-aligned `2px` vertical line in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Timeline Node**: Circular anchor (`36px × 36px`) using `#F8FAFC` surface, bound by a `2px` border corresponding to category color (Teal for Lab Reports, Violet for AI Syntheses, Amber for Diagnostics, Emerald for Wellness). Contains a clean category icon.
- **Timeline Event Card**: Nested adjacent to the node, featuring a timestamp, title, extracted highlights list, and attached record source.

### Report Comparison Matrix
- **Layout**: Two-column side-by-side or tabular grid comparing historical vs. current readings.
- **Change Indicators**: Arrows indicate direction alongside delta computation (e.g., `118 → 108 mg/dL`), backed by a non-judgmental trend label (`Decreased` / `Stable`).

### Form Inputs & Upload Zone
- **Input Fields**: Crisp `44px` height, `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Focus state upgrades to `1.5px solid #0D9488` with zero outer shadow blur.
- **Upload Zone**: Dashed 2px border using `#CBD5E1`, `#F8FAFC` surface. Transitions to `#F0FDFA` with `#14B8A6` border upon active drag-over.

### AI Processing Visual State
- Multi-step structured status list with animated checkmarks, subtle pulsing radial gradient nodes, and live tokenized text reveals instead of generic progress spinners.
